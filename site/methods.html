Methods & provenance

The replay has honesty rules of its own. They are enforced by validation, not just stated here.

The rules of this replay

  • Every stage marked as evidence-bound cites a frozen record with a SHA-256. No citation, no render — the validator fails the build.
  • Unresolved stages render as explicitly empty, labeled slots. There is no state that looks complete while no result exists.
  • Nothing is narrated as inevitable progress. The replay preserves the moments where the process did not know what would happen.
  • Historical verdicts and current standing are separate fields, dated separately.
  • The replay never invents earned / not earned — those come from the records' own claim boundaries.
  • No fixtures, mocks, or synthetic results are used to fill a missing scientific result. Ever.

What this repository is and isn’t

openline-rsi is a presentation layer. It renders frozen records from the scientific repositories. It is not a dependency of Airlock or of RSI-006, not an experiment runner, not an alternate receipt authority, and not a place that modifies or repairs historical evidence. The source repositories and their frozen records remain authoritative.

Evidence in this repo is a copy, made once and hash-pinned. Run the verification:

python3 tools/verify_evidence.py   # hashes every copied record, fails on mismatch
python3 tools/validate_replay.py   # enforces the data contract above

Provenance of the v0 evidence

Eighteen frozen records were copied from terryncew/openline-airlock (sixteen at main c82f0242e6556bbc8d920291f444090b526adeee; the Q6 preregistration and the Gate-2A NO-GO freeze record at main d3b29da9a83c8ee65ee095fae1b052460e08ece3) and two design drafts from the local workspace. The full manifest — source path, record type, digest, verification status — is site/data/manifest.json.

One record is referenced but not copied: the raw RIL_001R_RESULT.json bytes live only in a GitHub Actions artifact. The replay marks this as an external reference with its limitation stated, and shows the freeze record's terminal numbers instead of reconstructing the raw result.

Weaknesses, encoded rather than smoothed over

  • RSI-003's receipt embeds ephemeral /tmp paths. The replay presents hashes as the durable evidence, never the paths as inspectable locations.
  • RIL-001R's raw result bytes are off-workspace (artifact 10332197672). The replay dead-ends honestly at the hash.
  • RIL-001R's negative is arena-limited: both arms saturated at 24/24, and the formal verdict is INCONCLUSIVE on a measurement defect. Stated as “no advantage observed on a saturated arena” — never “the recursive arm lost”.
  • RSI-004's failure is a harness crash with no scientific verdict — shown as “crashed, no verdict”, not as a failed hypothesis.
  • The Q3 seal hashes elsewhere in the program are runner-printed lines only — “recorded, not relied upon”. They are not part of this replay.
  • The PLOS correction case in openline-claim-graph is partly authored (fixture keys, authored edges). It is a mechanism demonstration, not machine-generated experimental evidence, and has no place in a scientific slot.

Where future records plug in

  • RSI-006 terminal branch → recorded as its own lane in site/data/contrast.json, bound to the Q6 preregistration and the Gate-2A NO-GO freeze record. The RIL history is not replaced. The productivity result slot itself remains open: no experiment has answered the question, and none is currently authorized.
  • Independent replication → site/data/replication-*.json in the same replay schema, linked from the replicated stage.
  • Integrated correction → a new event in the top-level standing object of site/data/replay.json. The historical verdict stays frozen; the correction attaches after it.

Frozen records are cited, not paraphrased into stronger claims. If a sentence on this site outruns its evidence, that is a bug — report it against the record it cites.
