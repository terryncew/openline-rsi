OPENLINE RSI

A public record of recursive self-improvement.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AN AI INHERITS YESTERDAY’S ANSWER?

One version reaches a conclusion. Another inherits it.

Once something is inherited, it can stop feeling inherited. It just feels true.

The feeling doesn’t update when the evidence does.

A conclusion can outlive the reason anyone had for trusting it.

THE LESSON KEPT GOING.

The evidence behind one lesson was challenged.

The version that had already inherited it kept treating it as valid.

Nothing dramatic happened. That was the problem.

Recorded result: failed.

Show the record →

THEN THE DOUBT CAUGHT UP.

The setup was redesigned so a challenge to the evidence could travel forward with the lesson.

The checks blocked the next inheritance.

Recorded result: passed its test.

WHERE THE EVIDENCE ENDS.

That is what this experiment established.

When the reason for trusting a conclusion changes, that change does not have to disappear on the way forward.

The record ends there.

Which is the point of keeping one.

OpenLine Airlock.
The operational implementation.

OpenLine RSI renders frozen scientific records. It runs no experiments, modifies no evidence, and is not an authority over any result. The source repositories remain authoritative.

Verify the evidence yourself: python3 tools/verify_evidence.py from the repository root.
