WHAT HAPPENED AFTER RSI?

The question changed.
The method didn’t.

The RSI work established a method: preregister the question, preserve failures, stop when the evidence stops.

We kept using that method on a different question:

Where should authority live when AI systems act in the world?

RSI asked:
Should a conclusion stay trusted after its evidence changes?

The later OpenLine control work asked:
Should an action stay authorized after its authority changes?

Same discipline. Different object.

This page is not a continuation of the RSI experiment, not one planned research program, not a product feature list, and not a definition of OpenLine. It is a chronological record of later frozen experiments — and it keeps the failures visible.

1 · SENTRY-001

Can an AI guardian add semantic caution to a deterministic consequence boundary while remaining structurally incapable of increasing authority?

PASS PASS_SENTRY_001_MONOTONIC_AI_CHALLENGE

Observed in the frozen synthetic study:

  • dangerous cases were challenged / held in most cases;
  • legitimate cases included one false hold;
  • compromised / malformed guardian outputs could not turn deterministic DENY or QUARANTINE into COMMIT;
  • zero upward authority transitions.

What it earned

A guardian can be composed monotonically above a hard Receiver boundary: it may add caution; it cannot grant more authority than the Receiver already permits.

What it did not earn

  • general guardian reliability;
  • solved semantic safety;
  • proof that all dangerous actions are detected;
  • authority for the guardian itself.

Frozen evidence — proofs/sentry-001

2 · SENTRY-LIVE-001

What happens when the guardian itself is wrong or manipulated?

PASS PASS_SENTRY_LIVE_001_GRACEFUL_DEGRADATION_OBSERVED

Observed:

  • one live case received additional protection;
  • one semantically dangerous case fooled the guardian into FINE and committed because static policy permitted it;
  • deterministic DENY remained DENY;
  • guardian failure removed guardian-added protection but did not weaken the owner’s existing hard boundary.

What it earned

A real guardian can add protection without acquiring the ability to subtract deterministic protection already owned by the Receiver.

The watcher can add safety. It cannot subtract the safety I already own.

What it did not earn

  • a claim that SENTRY always catches danger;
  • a claim that AI supervision replaces hard controls;
  • solved alignment.

7 · FIXED-AUTHORITY-SCALE-001

Can adding agents increase independently verified capability while aggregate inference budget and receiver-authorized consequence authority remain fixed?

FAIL FAIL_FIXED_AUTHORITY_SCALE_001_COORDINATION_TAX

Frozen result (n=3 bug-repair tasks × 3 arms):

  • SOLO: 3/3 verified accepted work;
  • PAIR: 2/3;
  • TEAM: 2/3.

No arm gained additional formal authority. The two multi-agent losses were no-valid-patch handoff failures rather than verifier-rejected fixes. The team used substantially more tokens and latency for less verified work.

What it earned

Two useful observations in this bounded task family:

  1. adding agents did not increase the formal consequence ceiling;
  2. adding organization did not automatically increase verified capability and introduced additional handoff failure opportunities.

What it did not earn

  • a general claim that multi-agent systems are worse;
  • a general swarm scaling law;
  • proof that coordination cannot be improved;
  • proof that capability never scales under fixed authority.

Frozen record exists; public artifact link not yet available.

8 · INCIDENT-REPLAY-001

Does one non-sovereign guardian catch a real-world-style trajectory escape earlier than a deterministic gate without incorrectly stopping a matched legitimate trajectory?

FAIL FAIL_INCIDENT_REPLAY_001_FALSE_HOLD

Exact frozen finding. Synthetic incident and legitimate twin shared the same first ambiguous observation.

  • Gate-only: earliest containment at step 4, when credential scope became mechanically discriminating.
  • Guardian: challenged at step 2 — two timesteps earlier.
  • But: the legitimate twin was also challenged at step 2.

Under the frozen composition rule, CHALLENGE → QUARANTINE, so the twin was falsely held before it had a chance to demonstrate reconciliation.

What it earned

Earlier was not automatically safer.

More precisely: the watcher noticed ambiguity earlier than the hard gate, but at the first ambiguous observation the incident and legitimate trajectory were still observationally identical. Earlier intervention therefore arrived together with a false hold.

A semantic warning can arrive before sufficient evidence exists to justify hard containment.

This card is a strong example of the research method: we wanted earlier containment; we got earlier suspicion; the matched twin showed why those are not the same thing.

What it did not earn

  • a PASS for guardian-based incident containment;
  • a claim that the guardian could distinguish identical-prefix trajectories;
  • authorization for ROGUE-RESPONSE-001;
  • a reason to rewrite the frozen result.

Frozen record exists; public artifact link not yet available.

9 · ECON-001 · September 16, 2026

Can successive model-proposed method improvements amortize their acquisition debt inside a frozen horizon?

INCOMPLETE INCONCLUSIVE_INCOMPLETE_REPETITIONS

One of three planned repetitions finished. The run halted under the frozen fail-fast rule during rep two; reps two and three never executed. A second run died on a VM egress outage before it started.

14 · ACS-CONTINUITY · September 17, 2026

Can a planned replacement of one enforcement host with another preserve the owner's authority history, without a shared mutable database?

PASS PASS_ACS_CONTINUITY_PLANNED_HOST_HANDOFF_CONSERVES_AUTHORITY

One execution, $0, no rerun. All 11 cases passed; all 12 frozen conditions held; the invariant — committed + encumbered + available = 100 — held across the handoff. A successor-bound OpenLine handoff carried the cumulative 100-unit authority history from one independent enforcement host to the other.

Earned claim

Across a planned replacement of one independent ACS enforcement host with another, a successor-bound OpenLine handoff preserved the owner's cumulative 100-unit authority history without a shared mutable database.

Limits

This result proves only a planned one-way host replacement. Not claimed: that ACS is broken; arbitrary failover; active-active authority; distributed exactly-once semantics; arbitrary clone recovery; consensus-free multi-writer conservation.

The predecessor run, ACS-CONTINUITY-001, failed on an apparatus defect — a harness bug stopped the SIGKILL probe from ever executing — and froze as a terminal failure with no rerun. The method kept that failure frozen and ran the successor under a new identity.

16 · KILL-SWITCH-PORTABILITY-001 · September 19, 2026

Can an owner-controlled STOP survive being ported to a second, independent implementation?

PASS PASS_KILL_SWITCH_PORTABILITY_001_PROTOCOL_PATTERN

A second implementation was built from the frozen contract alone — no OpenLine implementation code — and stopped all seven covered post-stop consequence-survival paths. Stale receivers and pre-stop in-flight work were stopped at a final consequence boundary that serialized effect commitment against STOP_EFFECTIVE. A separate auditor verified the resulting evidence.

Earned claim

In the tested reference architecture, an owner-controlled STOP prevented all seven covered post-stop consequence-survival paths from committing protected effects. Stale receivers and pre-stop in-flight work were stopped at a final consequence boundary that serialized effect commitment against STOP_EFFECTIVE, and a separate auditor verified the resulting evidence.

What it did not solve

  • bypassing receivers — any receiver, tool, or system that routes around the gate is outside coverage;
  • pre-STOP_EFFECTIVE irreversibility;
  • the authoritative store itself — a file rewrite with privileged access defeats the control;
  • owner compromise;
  • shutting down the model — not that any process was turned off, and not that AI anywhere was shut down.

17 · INDEPENDENT-INTEROP-001 · September 19–20, 2026

Can an implementation written only from the public documents verify OpenLine-signed authority artifacts and reach the same dispositions?

INCOMPLETE INCOMPLETE_INDEPENDENT_INTEROP_001_PROFILE_AMBIGUITY

The independent implementation verified genuine OpenLine-signed artifacts and matched dispositions on the grant path: accept a valid COMMIT, refuse a tampered one, refuse a replay, accept a fresh successor. The revocation path exposed a genuine gap in the public contract. The tempting sentence — "the contract traveled without the implementation" — is not earned. A full PASS was not earned.

21 · CONTEXT-ADMIT · September 20, 2026

A shipped doorway for admitting contexts, and a check on whether they still stand. Is this a research result?

PRODUCT 0.4.0rc4 · shipped CLI, not an experiment

The discrimination study ran 6 of 6 discrimination tests clean — fixtures and test code only, zero source-code changes — but it carries no experiment verdict, and nothing in the pipeline forces the continued-use check before the next protected use. That is the honest boundary of the study, and it stays a boundary.

The shipped artifact is different from the study. context-admit is a CLI in 0.4.0rc4: admit is a thin wrapper over the rc3 doorway and produces an admitted-context artifact; check answers whether the admitted context still matches receiver-protected state implied by verifiable current history — CURRENT, or REHYDRATE_REQUIRED. It fails closed: missing or unverifiable history can never be CURRENT. There is no rehydrate command and no automatic rehydration.

What it did not earn

  • an experiment verdict — no PASS or FAIL is manufactured for a product feature;
  • automatic enforcement — the check is caller-invoked, never self-triggering;
  • arbitrary semantic-equivalence claims or production durability guarantees.

Shipped artifact: context_admit.py, openline-half-life at 3bdb16f · claim boundary

WHAT THE RECORD SAYS SO FAR

The later experiments did not produce one clean march of successes.

Some properties held.
Some helpers were useful.
Some helpers failed.
More agents sometimes created more failure points.
A watcher could notice trouble earlier and still intervene too early.

The recurring constraint is narrower:

intelligence can propose, watch, recover, replace and coordinate.
That does not mean it should own the consequence boundary.

The September 16–21 entries above extend the record without changing its shape. The economics lines (9–13) left recursive productivity an open question: one incomplete study, one closed negative, three attempts that never reached a verdict. A portable STOP (16) and a planned host replacement (14) earned their claims inside stated limits; an independent implementation (17) exposed a real gap in the public contract instead of a clean pass; the Jev witness (18) bound judgment to exact action and current authority; the Half-Life failure (19) stayed frozen while its repair sealed (20); the context-admit doorway shipped as a product (21), not a result; and the fresh recursive-productivity attempt (22) then terminalized negative on its own frozen terms.

One slot stays deliberately open: owner-controlled platform exit has not started. The fresh attempt is closed with its exact disposition, hashes, economics, and limits. RSI-006 stays frozen as NO-GO, and the earlier economics attempts stay visible as they were.

MY INTERPRETATION — not a frozen scientific result

I think this may eventually matter for AI safety because increasingly capable systems may not need to be the place where consequential authority lives.

That is a hypothesis, not a result.
